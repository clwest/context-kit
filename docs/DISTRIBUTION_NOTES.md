# Distribution Notes

Drafts for the launch push. Nothing here is finalized — every section
is a starting point that the maintainer should edit in their own voice
before publishing. Treat the wording as suggestions, not as ready-to-ship copy.

honest dogfood reports from people in the LinkedIn comment thread is

## Notes on the launch sequence

Suggested ordering, if it helps:

1. **Tuesday morning** — push the four+two new commits if not already.
2. **Tuesday afternoon** — reply to Damian, Brian, Austin under
   their original comments. Don't post anywhere new yet.
3. **Wednesday morning** — long-form LinkedIn update post (the one
   above).
4. **Wednesday or Thursday** — Twitter/X thread, links back to
   GitHub.
5. **Whenever ready** — PyPI publish. The tweet/post don't depend on
   it; "coming soon to PyPI" is fine messaging.

Don't batch all four into one day. You want a real chance to *read
and respond* to whatever comes back, especially dogfood reports.

## What we deliberately are not doing here

- Show HN. The repo is at v0.3.0 with one tagged release. Show HN is
  a swing once there's a real wheel install + a couple of public
  dogfood reports.
- Newsletter pitching. Same reason.
- A blog post on the technical design. The session handoffs in
  `docs/handoffs/` are essentially that, in working form.
- Asking people to "star" the repo. Stars don't help us learn what
  breaks.

The whole point of the launch sequence above is to convert the
~~232 impressions~~ + 12 comments on the original post into a
handful of actual users — not into a metrics screenshot.
